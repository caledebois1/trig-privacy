Trig privacy policy Last updated: June 2026 Trig stores your health data and check-ins on your iPhone only. • We do not collect, transmit, or receive any of your data. There is no account, no cloud storage, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. • Health data is read from Apple Health only with your permission, and only to show you your own information and patterns. It never leaves your device. • Your check-ins (mood, energy, medication, tags, notes) are stored locally and can be exported by you, for you, from the app's Settings. • Deleting the app deletes the data Trig holds. • TestFlight beta builds: Apple provides us basic, anonymous install and crash statistics through TestFlight. Screenshots and feedback you choose to send through TestFlight come to us with the device model and iOS version attached. • Trig provides behavioural observations from your own history. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Questions: geoff@wedgwoodassocs.com
